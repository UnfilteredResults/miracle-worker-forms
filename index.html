Welcome, Cast & Crew!

Thank you for being part of The Miracle Worker. To ensure everyone's safety and compliance with CLT policies, please complete all required paperwork below.

How it works:

  • Click on each form below to open it in a new window
  • Fill out all required fields (marked with *)
  • Sign using your mouse, trackpad, or finger (on touch devices)
  • Click Submit - the form will be emailed automatically to Mike Shipley
  • You'll receive a confirmation once your submission is received
