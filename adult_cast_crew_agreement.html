Adult Cast & Crew Member Agreement

Member Agreement

As a Cast or Crew Member of Clemson Little Theatre, I agree to:

  • Attend all scheduled rehearsals, tech rehearsals, and performances unless excused by the Director or Stage Manager
  • Be on time for all scheduled calls and remain until dismissed
  • Follow all safety protocols and guidelines established by CLT
  • Treat all cast, crew, staff, and volunteers with respect and professionalism
  • Take care of all costumes, props, and theatre property
  • Notify the Director or Stage Manager immediately if I am unable to attend a scheduled call
  • Refrain from the use of alcohol or illegal substances before or during rehearsals and performances
  • Support the artistic vision and decisions of the production team

I understand that:

  • My participation in this production is voluntary
  • Failure to comply with this agreement may result in removal from the production
  • Theatre activities involve some inherent risks, and I assume responsibility for my own safety
  • CLT reserves the right to use photographs and video recordings of rehearsals and performances for promotional purposes

Media Release

I grant Clemson Little Theatre permission to use photographs, video recordings, and audio recordings of me taken during rehearsals, performances, and related activities for promotional, educational, and archival purposes. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Social media posts (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.)
  • Website content and promotional materials
  • Print advertising and press releases
  • Video recordings for archival purposes

I understand that I will not receive compensation for the use of these materials and that all materials become the property of Clemson Little Theatre.
